          Tips

          • Resize: drag the thin dividers between cameras, between the videos and the map column, and between the map and G-sensor. Double-click a divider to reset it.
          • Rearrange: drag a camera's label onto another camera to swap them. Double-click a camera (or press 1–4) to set the main camera.
          • Timeline: drag to move through time. Ctrl + wheel (or the − and + buttons) zooms; the wheel alone scrolls. Each row is one camera: solid bars are driving, diagonal stripes are parking, vertical bars are event recordings. A filled triangle marks an event recording, an outlined triangle a firm or hard moment (0.3 g or more). The teal area is speed.
          • G-sensor: the dot behaves like a ball in a bowl, showing the force you feel: braking throws it up (forward), accelerating pushes it down (back), and a left turn throws it right. The two strips show the last and next 10 seconds.
          • Map: wheel to zoom, drag to pan, click on the route to jump to that moment.
          • Audio plays from one camera, picked in the speaker menu. While you drag the timeline you hear short snippets at the scrub position. Audio is muted above 4×.
          • 8× and 16× use keyframe stepping, so four 4K streams don't overload the decoder.
